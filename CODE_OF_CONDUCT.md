# Code of Conduct

AgentSkills welcomes contributors with different experiences, identities, and
levels of familiarity with the project. Help people participate and evaluate
their work fairly.

## Expected conduct

Be respectful and specific in technical discussions. Critique behavior and ideas,
explain evidence, accept good-faith corrections, and give credit. Respect privacy,
accessibility needs, and requests to stop an interaction.

Harassment, discriminatory language, threats, sexualized attention, doxxing,
impersonation, and sustained personal attacks are unacceptable. Do not publish
private messages or identifying information without permission.

## Scope and reporting

This policy applies in repository issues, pull requests, discussions, project
events, and other spaces where someone represents AgentSkills.

Report concerns privately to a listed repository maintainer through a private
contact published on their profile. Until the project establishes a dedicated
moderation address, do not send sensitive reports through public issues. Include
the relevant context, links, and the outcome you are seeking; redact unrelated
personal information. Maintainers should keep reports confidential to the extent
possible and avoid requiring the reporter to confront the person involved.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove harmful content and restrict participation.
Responses should reflect severity and recurrence: a correction or warning for
minor conduct, temporary restrictions for repeated or serious conduct, and a
permanent ban for threats or sustained abuse. Explain decisions privately when
appropriate. A maintainer involved in a report should recuse themselves; another
uninvolved maintainer should review any appeal where available.

A dedicated reporting channel and named moderation contacts must be established
by the repository owner before broad community promotion.
